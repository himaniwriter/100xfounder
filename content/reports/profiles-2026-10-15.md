## Notes and caveats

- **Few genuine comments.** Product Hunt flagged most comments on these launches as "Likely AI", so they were left out. Ghost Core and kodlo have only one or two short quotes each. HireKey has one. Perplexity Automations and Gboard have no opinions section.
- **Feature launches.** Perplexity Automations and Gboard Kurukuru launched under their parent company's Product Hunt page. Neither page shows that parent's Product Hunt rating, because the rating covers the whole company. Their launch stories are the hunter's comment, credited as "hunter".
- **Ghost Core availability.** The founder posted on X that the first batch had sold out, but ghost.ai still says it is open. The page sends readers to ghost.ai. The $11M seed figure is left out because only third-party posts give it.
- **Currency not named.** The pricing pages for kodlo (a Polish company) and HireKey show only "$". It is recorded as USD.
- **Maker name spelling.** Product Hunt displays kodlo's maker as "Andriana Oprsyko", but the username is andriana_oprysko. The page uses the displayed spelling.
- **Hardware schema.** Ghost Core and Gboard use `schema_category` "ComputerHardware" in the SoftwareApplication schema. That property is free text, but it is worth reviewing.
- **No logos.** None of the five has a logo. `content/fetch-startup-logos.py` would process every listing without one, not just these five, so it was not run.
