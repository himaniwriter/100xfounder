## 4. Cancellation and refunds — the single most valuable section

### Official cancellation [semrush.com/kb/252-cancelling-your-account, 4 Oct 2026]
1. Go to **semrush.com/cancel/** while logged in.
2. Complete **all** required fields (incomplete submissions may be denied).
3. You receive a confirmation email with a link **valid for 24 hours**.
4. **Click that link** or the request is not processed.
5. A support case is created.

**The 24-hour confirmation-link expiry is the mechanism behind essentially every "I cancelled and was still charged" complaint, and it appears on no page-1 competitor for any keyword.**

### Downgrade [kb/252 + kb/1013]
- **Not self-serve** — you must contact Support.
- No limit on the number of downgrades.
- Website monitoring data kept 30 days after downgrading.
- Upgrades *are* self-serve and prorated.

### Refunds [semrush.com/company/legal/refund-policy/, last updated 20 Aug 2025]
- One-time **7-day money-back guarantee**, only on (a) an **initial annual subscription of 12 months or longer**, or (b) initial add-on purchases.
- Verbatim: **"Refunds are not available for month-to-month subscriptions."**
- Must be **requested in writing in the comment field of the cancellation form** — cancelling alone does NOT trigger a refund.
- Processed within **30 calendar days**; **customer bears bank fees and FX charges**.
- Verbatim: *"Cancellation will take effect at the end of your committed subscription term or, for unpaid trial or free subscriptions, within 3 business days."*

### Data retention after cancelling [kb/252]
Account drops to free tier; monitoring folders visible but **locked**; **30-day grace period** to export; after that only the oldest folder's data is kept.

## 6. Data accuracy versus Ahrefs — don't pick a winner

Independent test, Ann Smarty [practicalecommerce.com, 12 Nov 2024], keyword "reddit marketing": **Google Ads 880,000 / Semrush 590,000 / Ahrefs 100,000** monthly US searches. Her conclusion, usable verbatim: *"keyword search volume is imprecise and inconsistent among the leading sources."* Treat volume as a **sorting signal, not a click forecast**.

Both vendors claim to win against different benchmarks; the studies are not comparable. Semrush claims "the most accurate Search Volume when compared to other industry-known tools" benchmarked against GSC impressions [semrush.com/blog/us-search-volume-update/].

One original test: bloggingx.com [31 Dec 2025] found GSC 3.71k clicks vs Semrush 2.1k estimate = **43% underestimate**; under-estimation by site size: <10k/mo 40–60%, 10k–100k 30–45%, 100k+ ~30%. Semrush volumes are **12-month averages**, so they lag trending and AI topics.

Vendor-claimed index sizes (neither independently verified): Semrush keyword DB 26.1bn vs Ahrefs 8bn; backlinks Semrush 43tn vs Ahrefs 35tn.

User-review consensus: **Ahrefs** credited with backlink index quality; **Semrush** with breadth of toolset. Capterra: Ahrefs 4.7/5 (585 reviews) vs Semrush 4.6/5 (2,329). Semrush specifically dinged on PPC data accuracy.
