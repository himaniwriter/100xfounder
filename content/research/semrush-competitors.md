## 0. The single most important finding: Semrush restructured its plans in 2026

The brief (`content/SEMRUSH-BRIEF.md`) is written around **Pro / Guru / Business**. Those plan
names are **no longer on Semrush's own pricing page**. The current ladder is
**Free / SEO / Starter / Pro+ / Advanced / Enterprise**.

This invalidates section 3 of the brief as written ("Pro vs Guru vs Business") and most of the
competitor price comparisons that circulate online. It is also a large content-gap opportunity:
most ranking pages still describe the retired ladder.

## 1. Corporate fact: Adobe now owns Semrush (verified, primary)

- Adobe **completed** its acquisition of Semrush Holdings, Inc. on **28 April 2026** for
  **$1.87 billion, primarily in cash**; Semrush's results are consolidated into Adobe from the
  acquisition date. Adobe states the deal adds "solutions for search engine optimization,
  generative engine optimization and agentic search optimization."
  [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000796343/000079634326000112/adbe-20260529.htm
  (Adobe Form 10-Q, FY2026 Q2), checked 4 Oct 2026]
- Semrush was subsequently delisted from the NYSE and became a wholly owned Adobe subsidiary —
  **SECONDARY** [search results incl. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semrush, 4 Oct 2026]. Low-risk claim
  but worth a primary citation (Adobe press release or Form 25) before publishing.

This is strong, citable, primary material and almost certainly under-covered by the pages
currently ranking. It is also the honest frame for "what happens to the price next?" — which is
the question buyers actually have in 2026.

**Claim to treat with caution:** several third-party blogs state Semrush "retired the
Pro/Guru/Business ladder in **May 2026**, shortly after the Adobe deal closed." The *outcome* is
verified (those plans are gone from Semrush's pricing pages). The *date* and the *causal link to
Adobe* are **UNVERIFIED** and should not be asserted.
