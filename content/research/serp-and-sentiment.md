**Compiled 4 October 2026.** Companion to `content/research/semrush-competitors.md` (which covers the
2026 plan restructure and the Adobe deal). This file does **not** repeat what that file already
verified; it covers Jobs A (SERP gaps), B (sentiment) and C (primary-page gap fills).

Every claim carries `[source URL, date checked]`. Labels used:

- **PRIMARY VERIFIED** — read directly on semrush.com / its help centre / an official newsroom page.
- **SECONDARY** — third-party page only. Re-verify before publishing as fact.

# PART C FIRST — primary-source gap fills

Put first because several Part A/B findings only make sense against it.

## C1. The plan ladder: Semrush's own pages now contradict each other

This is the single most useful finding in this file.

| Source | Plan names published | Prices |
|---|---|---|
| `semrush.com/pricing/` | **Free / SEO / Starter / Pro+ / Advanced / Enterprise** — no Pro, Guru or Business anywhere | $0 / $139 / $199 / $299 / $549 / custom (monthly); $117.33 / $165.17 / $248.17 / $455.67 annual-equivalent |
| `semrush.com/kb/1547-seo-toolkit-pricing-limits` | **SEO Toolkit Pro / Guru / Business** — still live, still priced | $139.95 / $249.95 / $499.95 per month |

[https://www.semrush.com/pricing/, checked 4 Oct 2026 — **PRIMARY VERIFIED**]
[https://www.semrush.com/kb/1547-seo-toolkit-pricing-limits, checked 4 Oct 2026 — **PRIMARY VERIFIED**]

So the honest statement is **not** "Pro/Guru/Business were retired". It is:

> As of 4 October 2026 Semrush's public pricing page sells only Free, SEO, Starter, Pro+, Advanced and
> Enterprise. The old Pro/Guru/Business ladder is no longer offered there, but Semrush's own help
> centre still publishes those three plans with prices and limits
> [semrush.com/pricing/ and semrush.com/kb/1547-seo-toolkit-pricing-limits, both checked 4 Oct 2026].
> Existing subscribers should check their own Subscription Info rather than trust any article.

The KB page carries **no last-updated date** [kb/1547, 4 Oct 2026], so we cannot say whether it is a
stale page or a still-sellable legacy track. **Do not assert either.** Phrase it as the contradiction
it is — that is more useful to a buyer than a guess, and nothing else on page 1 does it.

**PRIMARY VERIFIED, but only for the legacy names** [kb/1547, 4 Oct 2026]:

| Limit | Pro | Guru | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Results per report | 10,000 | 30,000 | 50,000 |
| Websites to monitor | 5 | 15 | 40 |
| Keywords to track | 500 | 1,500 | 5,000 |
| Site Audit pages crawled / month | 100,000 | 300,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Keyword metric updates / month | 250 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| SEO Ideas / month | 500 | 800 | 2,000 |

**Results-per-report figures for the NEW SEO / Starter / Pro+ / Advanced tiers are NOT published on
`semrush.com/pricing/` or `semrush.com/prices/`** [both checked 4 Oct 2026]. Neither page states any
rows-per-report or export-row limit. **UNVERIFIED — do not state a number for the new tiers.**

The website and keyword-tracking counts do map across cleanly, which is useful and citable:

old Guru limits at $299 vs $249.95, and Advanced carries old Business limits at $549 vs $499.95 — a
roughly $49/month uplift for the same hard limits, plus the AI-visibility features. That arithmetic is
ours, derived from two primary pages, and is safe to publish as such.

## C3. Free trial on the MAIN plans — yes, and it is seven days

**PRIMARY VERIFIED.** `semrush.com/pricing/` states:

> "Try Semrush free for seven days. Cancel anytime."

[https://www.semrush.com/pricing/, checked 4 Oct 2026]

On `semrush.com/prices/` the SEO, Starter and Pro+ cards each carry a **"Try for free"** call to
action; the **Advanced** card offers subscribe only — no trial button
[https://www.semrush.com/prices/, checked 4 Oct 2026 — **PRIMARY VERIFIED**]. Worth flagging: the
most expensive mainstream tier is the one you cannot trial.

Trial terms, from the help centre [https://www.semrush.com/kb/1011-subscriptions, 4 Oct 2026 —
**PRIMARY VERIFIED**], quoted:

- "Each toolkit trial can be activated once per account"
- "You can cancel at any time before the trial ends"
- "After your 7-day trial, your account converts to the plan you selected unless you cancel"
- **"Exporting data is disabled during the trial period"** ← this is the one almost nobody mentions
- Advertising Toolkit and AI PR add-ons carry their own 7-day trials, "no cost or commitment"
  [https://www.semrush.com/kb/1551-pricing-and-plans, 4 Oct 2026]

**The "14-day trial" is not a Semrush offer.** Affiliate pages widely advertise 14 days on
"Semrush One", "Pro" or "Guru" — e.g. joinsecret.com offers an "exclusive 14-day free trial" on
"Pro or Guru" and lists plans as Free/Pro $139/Guru $249/Business $499
[https://www.joinsecret.com/semrush, page last modified 3 Oct 2026, checked 4 Oct 2026 —
**SECONDARY**]; demandsage.com's page is titled "14 Days Guru, One & Pro"
[https://www.demandsage.com/semrush-free-trial/, updated 1 Oct 2026 — **SECONDARY**]. Semrush's own
page says seven. We should say seven, cite the page, and note that longer trials are partner offers
whose terms we cannot verify. **We must not claim a 14-day trial ourselves.**

## C4. Cancellation — the live URL, and the step everyone misses

The old `kb/998-how-to-cancel-subscription` 404s. **The current article is
`https://www.semrush.com/kb/252-cancelling-your-account`** [checked 4 Oct 2026 —
**PRIMARY VERIFIED**]. There is also a dedicated form at `https://www.semrush.com/cancel/`.

Steps, as Semrush documents them [kb/252, 4 Oct 2026]:

1. Log in, then submit the request via the Cancellation Form (`semrush.com/cancel/`).
2. Complete all the questions in the form.
3. **Click the confirmation link emailed to you.** Quoted: "You'll receive a cancellation confirmation
   email with a confirmation link that is **valid for 24 hours**." Miss that window and the
   cancellation does not process. Check spam/junk.
4. "To prevent future renewal charges, cancel your subscription before your next renewal."

After cancelling: "After canceling your subscription or trial, you will still be able to access
Semrush on a free-level account." Website Monitoring folders lock but stay visible, with a "30-day
grace period during which we will save your Website Monitoring data" [kb/252, 4 Oct 2026].

**Downgrade:** there is no self-serve path. Quoted: "To downgrade to another pricing plan, simply
contact Support, and we'll take care of it." [kb/252, 4 Oct 2026]. Data is kept 30 days if you
re-upgrade; one month if you downgrade to free (oldest folder retained).

## C5. Refund policy

**PRIMARY VERIFIED** [https://www.semrush.com/company/legal/refund-policy/ and
https://www.semrush.com/refund, both checked 4 Oct 2026; summarised at kb/252]:

- A **one-time, 7-day money-back guarantee**.
- Limited to **initial** subscription purchases where the committed term is **twelve months or
  greater**, and to initial add-on purchases incremental to an existing subscription.
- **Month-to-month subscriptions are not eligible. Renewals are not eligible.**
- Must be purchased **directly from Semrush.com using a credit card**.
- Written request within **7 calendar days** of the order.
- Processed within **30 calendar days** to the original payment method.
- Mechanically: submit a cancellation request and state that you want a refund.

This matters enormously for the page: **the single most common complaint in user reviews (Part B) is
people on monthly plans expecting a refund and not getting one — which is exactly what the policy
says.** We can be genuinely useful here: tell readers to trial, then if they buy annual, decide inside
7 days; and that a monthly plan buys flexibility but zero refund rights.

# PART A — SERP gap analysis

**Method note and caveat.** I could not capture live Google SERPs with ranking positions or the
literal "People also ask" box. What follows is built from WebSearch result sets (which approximate the
page-1 field) plus direct reads of the ranking pages. **The ranking *order* below is not verified
position data** — treat it as "appears in the page-1 field". The PAA-style question sets are taken from
the FAQ sections and H2s of the pages that actually rank, which is a defensible proxy and arguably more
actionable. All searches run 4 Oct 2026.

## The pattern across all eight keywords

Before the per-keyword detail, the structural finding — this is the thesis for the whole article:

1. **Almost no page on page 1 handles the plan-ladder situation correctly.** Pages fall into three
   buckets: (a) still selling Pro/Guru/Business as current; (b) only the new ladder, with no mention
   that the old one existed or that legacy subscribers exist; (c) both, presented as two parallel
   product lines without saying which a new buyer can actually purchase today. **Nobody surfaces the
   contradiction between semrush.com/pricing and Semrush's own KB 1547** (finding C1).
2. **Price figures on page 1 are inconsistent and frequently wrong.** On one keyword I saw $139,
   $139.95, $177.33 and $199 all presented as the entry price, across four pages, in the same week.
3. **The "14-day free trial" claim is near-universal on affiliate pages and contradicts semrush.com.**
4. **The affiliate field is thin and newly spun.** Consistent with the earlier research run, the
   pricing field in particular is crowded with sites with no brand or author track record —
   contextbolt.com, allable.ai, justpricing.com, criticnest.com, toolsurf.com, resourcera.com,
   smartguidehubs.com, innovatehubfinance.com, top50aitools.com, reddily.io, hackceleration.com,
   ai-cmo.net [WebSearch result sets, 4 Oct 2026].
5. **The Adobe acquisition is almost entirely absent from the review pages**, even ones updated after
   the close. explodingtopics.com's review (updated 2 Mar 2026) does not mention it at all
   [https://explodingtopics.com/blog/semrush-review, 4 Oct 2026]. For a buyer in Oct 2026, "who owns
   this now and what does that mean for my renewal?" is a live question nobody on page 1 answers.
6. **Nobody links the refund policy to the complaint pattern.** The reviews are full of people who
   could not get refunds; the policy plainly excludes monthly plans and renewals. No ranking page I
   read joins those two facts.

**Unanswered questions we can own:**
1. A current, dated, side-by-side price table with the *actual* 2026 plan names on both sides. The

4. AI/LLM visibility head-to-head: Semrush One's prompt tracking (50/100/200 prompts a day by tier,
