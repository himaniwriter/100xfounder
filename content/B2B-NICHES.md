# B2B lead-gen listing niches: truss, skincare, fragrance (planning pass)

Prepared 2026-10-04. Planning document, not article copy. Each listing article ends in a "Request quotes" form.

## How to read this

- **Demand estimate** is an *estimate*, not search volume. It comes from three signals gathered on 2026-10-04: (a) whether the exact phrase appears in Google autocomplete for India (`gl=in`); (b) whether IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial or ExportersIndia run a category or city page for it; (c) how many competitor "top N" listing articles exist. **High** means all three signals are present and the phrase leads autocomplete. **Med** means it appears in autocomplete, usually as a city or modifier variant. **Low** means it only shows up as a long-tail or adjacent query. Check volumes in GSC or Keyword Planner before scaling.
- **Intent:** B = buyer (brand founder, event company, procurement), I = informational, J = job seeker. Autocomplete showed J leakage only on "skincare manufacturing jobs", so avoid "manufacturing company" titles that read like job searches.
- **List size** is how many *genuine manufacturers* (own facility claimed, official website, identifiable registered business) we can expect to verify. Many IndiaMART "manufacturers" are actually traders or fabricators. Every entry needs a check against its own site plus MCA/GST or licence evidence in the writing phase.
- **Priority:** 1 = write first, 2 = next wave, 3 = later or supporting.

### Competition pattern seen across all three niches
The SERPs are dominated by (1) IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial and ExportersIndia directory pages, and (2) **self-serving "Top 10" blog posts written by manufacturers that list themselves at #1** (for example, Baddi and Ahmedabad lists on hcpwellness.in, orchidlifesciences.com, arisecosmetic.com and lifevisionhealthcare.in, and the private-label perfume list on primacyind.com). There are almost no neutral, sourced, editorially independent lists. That gap is our angle: a transparent "how we verified" box, a licence or standard column per company, no pay-to-rank (sponsored slots clearly labelled per CLAUDE.md), and one quote form.

## Regulatory notes for buyers (with sources)

### Skincare / cosmetics (India)
- **Law:** Cosmetics are regulated under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and the **Cosmetics Rules, 2020** (in force from 15 Dec 2020). Policy sits with CDSCO; **manufacturing licences are issued by the State Licensing Authority** (state drugs controller). Sources: [CDSCO Cosmetics page](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Cosmetics/) · [Cosmetics Rules 2020 (CDSCO PDF)](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/cos_rules/Cosmetics%20Rules%202020.pdf) · [CDSCO cosmetics FAQ](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/cosmetics/Frequently-Asked-Questions-FAQcosmeticia.pdf)
- **Manufacturing licence:** apply on **Form COS-5**, licence granted on **Form COS-8**. Under the **loan-licence route**, used by brands that have products made at someone else's licensed plant, the brand applies on **Form COS-6** and is granted **Form COS-9**. Licences are decided within 45 days and stay valid indefinitely if the retention fee is paid every 5 years. Buyer tip: ask the manufacturer for its COS-8 copy and confirm your product category is endorsed on it.
- **Portal:** state licences are processed through **ONDLS** ([statedrugs.gov.in](https://statedrugs.gov.in/)). Import registration (COS-1/COS-2) goes through CDSCO's SUGAM portal.
- **Animal testing:** Rule 39 of the Cosmetics Rules 2020 prohibits testing cosmetics on animals in India, and a self-declaration is required at licensing. Sources: Rules PDF above; [PETA India summary](https://www.petaindia.com/blog/peta-india-applauds-new-cosmetics-rules-for-strengthening-import-ban-on-animal-tested-cosmetics/)
- **Labelling and specs:** the Rules set mandatory label particulars and require products to conform to the Ninth Schedule (BIS) specifications. Packaged-goods declarations also fall under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (cite the official text when writing).
- **Ayurvedic/herbal caveat (Haridwar, Kerala lists):** products sold as *Ayurvedic* fall under the AYUSH licensing provisions of the Drugs Rules, not the cosmetic COS-8 route. Tell buyers to check which licence matches their claims. Confirm the exact form and rule from the official AYUSH/Drugs Rules text before publishing.

### Perfume / fragrance
- **Perfumes, deodorants and attars are "cosmetics"** in India, so the same COS-8 / COS-9 manufacturing licence applies (sources as above).
- **Alcohol-based perfumes:** the **Medicinal and Toilet Preparations (Excise Duties) Act, 1955** covers toilet preparations containing alcohol, and its definition expressly includes perfumes and deodorants. State excise departments issue **L-1 (manufacture in bond) / L-2 (outside bond)** licences. Sources: [Delhi Excise, Medicinal & Toiletry Preparations](https://excise.delhi.gov.in/excise/medicinal-and-toiletry-preparations) · [Tamil Nadu: Act & Rules PDF](https://www.cpe.tn.gov.in/pdf/l1.pdf). Buyer tip: an EDP/EDT private-label partner should hold both the cosmetic licence and the state excise licence. Attars are oil-based and typically fall outside this, so state the distinction carefully.
- **IFRA Standards:** these are global self-regulatory limits on fragrance ingredients (restricted or prohibited materials by product category). The **51st Amendment** was notified 30 June 2023, and the last deadline for existing creations was 30 Oct 2025. The **52nd Amendment** consultation closed 12 June 2026; IFRA says notification is expected around **January 2027**, entering into force 11 months later for new creations and 30 months later for existing ones. Buyer tip: ask for an **IFRA Certificate of Conformity** per fragrance and the product category it covers. Sources: [IFRA 51st Amendment notification letter (PDF)](https://d3t14p1xronwr0.cloudfront.net/docs/Standards-Documentation/ifra-51st-amendment-notification-letter.pdf) · [IFRA 51 guidance (PDF)](https://d3t14p1xronwr0.cloudfront.net/docs/Standards-Documentation/ifra-51st-amendment-guidance-for-the-use-of-ifra-standards.pdf) · [IFRA: end of consultation, 52nd Amendment](https://ifrafragrance.org/latest-updates/ifra-news/ifra-publishes-end-of-consultation-letter-for-the-52nd-amendment-to-the-ifra-standards)
- **Kannauj GI:** "Kannauj Perfume" is a registered Geographical Indication. Sources: [IP India GI registry, application 157](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublic/Application/Details/157) · [DC-MSME Kannauj Attar & Essential Oil cluster diagnostic report (PDF)](https://www.dcmsme.gov.in/tcsp/Program%20Overview/Kannauj_V1.pdf), which reports roughly 375 attar and essential-oil units. Avoid claiming any specific firm is "GI-authorised" unless it is on the registered-user list.
- **UAE angle:** UAE products need emirate-level and federal approvals, such as Dubai Municipality product registration. Research the official source when writing F8 rather than relying on manufacturer claims.

### Truss
- **Entertainment (aluminium) truss:** India has no truss-specific BIS standard. Credible manufacturers cite **ANSI E1.2-2021** (design, manufacture and use of aluminium trusses and towers; free from ESTA TSP) and/or **EN 17115:2018** (design and manufacture of aluminium and steel trusses for entertainment). They publish **load-span tables** (UDL, centre-point, third- and quarter-point loads) and use alloy **EN AW-6082-T6** tube. Outdoor temporary structures: **ANSI E1.21**. Buyer tip: ask for load tables, alloy certificates and welder qualification, and treat any seller without load tables as a red flag. Sources: [ESTA TSP published/free standards](https://tsp.esta.org/tsp/documents/published_docs.php) · [ANSI E1.2-2021 (ANSI webstore)](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/esta/ansie12021-2440346) · [BS EN 17115:2018 (BSI)](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/entertainment-technology-specifications-for-design-and-manufacture-of-aluminium-and-steel-trusses) · [Example load-span tables, Global Truss F34 (PDF)](https://www.globaltruss.com/pub/media/categorycustomlink/pdf/F34_Truss_Load_Span_Tables.pdf)
- **Indian aluminium structural code:** **IS 8147** (aluminium alloys in structures). The 1976 text is public; a 2023 limit-state revision is reported, so confirm on the BIS store before citing. Source: [IS 8147:1976 (public.resource.org)](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.8147.1976.pdf)
- **Roof truss / PEB (steel):** design to **IS 800:2007** (general construction in steel, limit state) with loads from **IS 875 Parts 1–3** (dead, imposed, wind; Part 3 revised 2015) and seismic loads from IS 1893. **SP 38 (1987)** gives typified roof-truss designs. Buyer tip: ask for stamped structural drawings citing IS 800/IS 875 and mill test certificates. Sources: [SP 38:1987 (public.resource.org)](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.sp.38.1987.pdf). Cite IS 800 and IS 875 from the official BIS standards portal when writing.

## Research method and limits
- Autocomplete was pulled from Google's suggest endpoint (`gl=in`, `hl=en`) on 2026-10-04 for about 38 seed phrases. It shows that queries exist and roughly how they rank, not their volume.
- Competitor and directory checks were done through web search result sets, not full SERP scraping, and no manufacturer was contacted. The company names above are **candidates to verify**, not endorsements. Some come from self-promotional lists, and some (especially on IndiaMART) are traders.
- No personal contact details were collected.
