# Calculator keyword research (2026-10-20)

**Sources:**
- Google autocomplete, India and US: 43 seeds, 606 real queries (`autocomplete.json`)
- Google Trends, last 12 months, worldwide and India

There is no Semrush or Ahrefs data. Shared-account Semrush mirrors were refused as a security risk. Difficulty below is judged from who usually ranks on page 1.

## What the trends say

- **Founder tools, worldwide:** runway calculator leads (average about 37), cap table calculator is second (about 13). ESOP, startup valuation and equity dilution calculators are close to 0.
- **AI cost tools, worldwide:** "token calculator" is about 4× runway. It peaked around May 2026 and is still large. Part of that interest is crypto and gaming tokens, so target the AI-specific variants. LLM cost, AI ROI and GPU cost are small but steady.
- **India:** salary hike calculator dominates (average about 67) and token calculator is strong (about 31). The exact phrase "ctc to in hand calculator", the notice period calculator and the ESOP calculator are tiny. Indians search "in hand salary calculator" or "salary calculator" instead.
