Which Semrush ladder, and which rung

Work down the questions. Stop at the first “yes”. All figures are monthly list prices.
QUESTION 1
Do you need programmatic / API access?
→ One Advanced $549, or Business $499.95 if the help-centre ladder is still sold. Or look at Ahrefs, Serpstat or SpyFu, which include API lower down.
QUESTION 2
More than 5 sites, or more than 500 tracked keywords?
→ One Pro+ $299. 15 sites, 1,500 keywords, 100 daily AI prompts.
QUESTION 3
Do you want any AI-search visibility tracking?
→ One Starter $199. Never stack the standalone toolkit on the entry plan.
$139.95 Pro + $99 AI Visibility = $238.95
One Starter = $199
$39.95/mo cheaper — $479.40 a year
QUESTION 4
Classic SEO only, one site, no AI?
→ One SEO $139. It is the cheapest paid entry Semrush publishes.
The two ladders map 1:1
Pro $139.95≈SEO $139
Guru $249.95≈Pro+ $299
Business $499.95≈Advanced $549
Pro+ gives the same sites, keywords and crawl allowances Guru gave. The difference in price is roughly $49/mo for the AI layer — our own arithmetic from Semrush’s two pricing pages.
Whether a new customer can still buy Pro, Guru or Business is not something we could verify. Ask support in writing before you plan around it.
