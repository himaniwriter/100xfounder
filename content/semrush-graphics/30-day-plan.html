First 30 days · a plan, not a tour

Get your money back out of month one

One deliverable per week. If week 1 produces nothing you can act on, cancel before the renewal rather than hoping month two is better.
DAYS 1–7
Baseline and bugs
Run Site Audit on the whole site. Export the crawl before you do anything else.

Set Position Tracking on the 100 keywords that already earn money, not the 500 you wish you ranked for.

Connect Google Search Console so you can compare Semrush estimates with your real clicks.
Deliverable: a ranked list of technical fixes.
DAYS 8–14
Where the demand is
Keyword Magic on your three core topics. Filter by intent, then by difficulty, then export.

Keyword Gap against your two closest competitors. The “missing” and “weak” tabs are the whole point.

Treat volumes as a sorting signal. They are 12-month averages and they disagree with Google Ads.
Deliverable: 20 briefs, ordered.
DAYS 15–21
Links and AI answers
Backlink Gap against the same two competitors. Export the domains that link to both of them and not to you.

Backlink Audit on your own profile. Look at what is new and what is lost, not the toxicity score.

On Starter and above, set up AI prompt tracking for the ten questions a buyer would actually type.
Deliverable: an outreach list and an AI baseline.
DAYS 22–30
Decide, and diarise
Export everything you want to keep. Exports are what you lose first.

Price your real bill: base plan, every seat, every extra domain, every location.

Put a reminder in the calendar five days before renewal. If you are cancelling, allow for the 24-hour email confirmation.
Deliverable: keep, downgrade or cancel.
