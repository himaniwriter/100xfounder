Pick Semrush for breadth, for AI-visibility tracking inside the subscription, and because you can trial it before paying.
Pick Ahrefs for backlink work, crawl scale, published row limits and API access you do not have to buy the top tier to get.
Do not pick on volume accuracy. One published test put “reddit marketing” at 880,000 in Google Ads, 590,000 in Semrush and 100,000 in Ahrefs. Treat volume as a sorting signal.
