How to cancel · semrush.com/kb/252 + refund policy

Cancelling takes five steps, not one

Step 4 is the one people miss. Without it the request is never processed and the renewal goes through.
1
Log in and go to semrush.com/cancel/. The dashboard’s own subscription screen is not the cancellation route.
2
Complete every required field. Semrush states incomplete submissions may be denied.
3
If you want a refund, ask for it in the comment field. Cancelling alone does not trigger one.
4
Open the confirmation email and click the link within 24 hours. It expires. Miss it and nothing is cancelled.
5
A support case opens. Screenshot the confirmation and keep the email.
The refund rules, exactly
Window7 days, once per account
EligibleAn initial annual subscription of 12 months or longer, or an initial add-on purchase
Not eligibleMonth-to-month subscriptions — never
How to requestIn writing, in the comment field of the cancellation form
ProcessingWithin 30 calendar days
Who pays the feesYou do — bank fees and currency-exchange charges
Also worth knowing
Downgrading is not self-serve. You have to contact support. Upgrades are self-serve and prorated.

After cancelling, the account drops to free. Monitoring folders stay visible but locked, and you get a 30-day window to export.
