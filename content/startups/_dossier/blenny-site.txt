 Slope · 20 – 35 m · Organize
 Drag. Apply. Undo if you change your mind.
 Draft first. Drag icons between Surface, Reef and Deep. Nothing touches your menu bar until you choose Apply.
 One-step Undo. Undo Changes reverses the last Apply, visibility and order together.
 Stop means stop. Stop or Quit releases Blenny's restrictions at once. Resume picks your saved setup back up.
 No mouse required. Every icon has context-menu and accessibility actions for moving it.

 Deep · below 35 m · Always hidden
 Some things belong below the reef. 
 Deep apps stay out of the menu bar and out of every reveal session. They keep running and stay yours; they just stop asking for attention. Move them back up whenever you like.
 48 m · Built to be careful
 Gentle with your Mac. 
 Hands off your pointer
 Blenny never moves the cursor, clicks for you or fakes a Command‑drag.
 No Screen Recording
 Blenny doesn't watch your screen, so it never asks for Screen Recording permission.
 One careful writer
 Every change runs through a single queue with a saved recovery record, one verification and at most one retry.
 Fails closed
 If the menu bar changed under it, Blenny stops and asks instead of writing again.
 Recoverable
 Interrupted changes keep a recovery record, so Recover Changes can finish or restore them.
 Signed updates
 Updates arrive through Sparkle, checked against Blenny's own key. Automatic checks stay off until you turn them on.
 52 m · Honest charts
 What Blenny can't reach yet. 
 Clock and Notification Center. While Blenny manages the menu bar, clicking the clock can't open Notification Center. Swipe left from the right edge of the trackpad instead.
 Now Playing, Gaming and Wine. These system extras may disappear during management. Stop brings them back.
 Some system items stay put. Siri, Time Machine and Control Center can't be sorted yet. Clock and the overflow arrow are fixed.
 macOS has the last word. On a narrow screen the system's own overflow can still tuck away a Surface app.
 Tested ground. Verified on macOS 27.0 (26A428) with Apple silicon. Other builds and display setups haven't been verified.
 56 m · Install
 Four steps to the reef. 
 Download the disk image from GitHub Releases .
 Drag Blenny into Applications , eject the disk image and open the installed copy.
 Allow the first launch. Blenny is self-signed and not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security ( Apple's guide ).
 Grant Device Control when Blenny asks. That's it: no Screen Recording, no SIP changes.

 58 m · Questions
 Before you dive in. 
 Is Blenny free? Yes. Blenny is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license. If it earns a place in your menu bar, you can support it through GitHub Sponsors or Ko‑fi.
 Why is it called Blenny? Blennies are small reef fish that live in holes and crevices and poke their heads out to watch the world. That is exactly how a Reef app should behave.
 Which Macs does it support? macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon. There is no macOS 26 or Intel version.
 What's the difference between Reef and Deep? Reef apps come up whenever you click the fish for a reveal session. Deep apps never do; they stay out of the menu bar until you move them to another area.
 How do updates work? Use Check for Updates in Settings or in Blenny's menu. Automatic checks are off by default, and turning them on still doesn't install anything unattended.
 How do I uninstall it? Finish or recover any running change, choose Stop, turn off Open Blenny at Login and quit. Then move Blenny to the Trash. The recovery and uninstall guide covers the details.
 Something went wrong. Now what? Choose Stop to release Blenny's restrictions right away. If Blenny reports an unfinished change, use Recover Changes and keep its recovery data until it completes. Reproducible problems belong in GitHub Issues .
