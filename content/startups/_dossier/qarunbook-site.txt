 From “where do I start?” to a clear testing process. 
 An app has plenty of things to check. Deciding what matters, explaining how to test it, and following up on problems can become a job of its own.
 qarunbook brings that work together, so you can get on with testing.
 0 1
 Know what to check.
 Create your own test plan or get AI to help. Give each check clear steps and an expected result, so testing has a useful starting point.
 0 2
 Work through it together.
 Test on your own, bring in teammates, or connect your AI assistant. Keep the plan and results in one place as the work gets done.
 0 3
 Give problems a proper follow-up.
 Keep issues connected to the checks that found them. When something is fixed, check it again before calling it passed.
 Built around how testing actually happens. On your own, as a team, or with AI. 
 Whoever is doing the testing, the plan, the results and the follow-up stay in one place.
 Problems stay with their checks
 Every issue sits on the check that found it, with the platforms it affects. Raising one takes the pass away; when it’s fixed, the check waits for someone to look again.
 The right access for each person
 Someone can run one app and only test another. People see just the projects you give them, and a join link brings a whole group in at once.
 A plan you can shape
 Run /test-checklist and your AI drafts the checks from your app. Edit them, add your own, choose the platforms, or start from scratch.
 Every app in one place
 Each app keeps its own sections and platforms, so a web app, a driver app and a marketing site can all be tested side by side.
 Connect your AI in about a minute. 
 qarunbook works over MCP, so the assistant you already use can read the plan, help with testing, record results, and follow up on what it fixes.
