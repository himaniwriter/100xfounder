Nithin Kamath bootstrapped Zerodha: a stock broker built without outside investors.

→ He started trading stocks at about 17, in the late 1990s (Business Today)
→ After the dotcom bust he spent four years at a call centre, making calls to people in the US, in his own words (Business Today, May 2024)
→ He bootstrapped Zerodha, which began operations on 15 August 2010 and pioneered discount broking in India (Zerodha)
→ In the early days it ran on NSE NOW, the exchange's free trading platform, and NSE waived its ~₹5 lakh membership fee (Nithin Kamath's blog, Sep 2026)
→ Equity delivery is ₹0 brokerage; intraday and F&O orders cost a flat ₹20 or 0.03%, whichever is lower (Zerodha)
→ Zerodha says it has 1.8 crore+ clients and over 15% of Indian retail trading volumes (Zerodha, accessed Oct 2026)

His rule for money: "Boring is usually better."
