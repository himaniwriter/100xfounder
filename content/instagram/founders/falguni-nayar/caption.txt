She started Nykaa at 49, after 18 years in investment banking.

→ Falguni Nayar spent 18 years at Kotak Mahindra Bank, where she was managing director of the investment bank (Fortune)
→ As a banker she took companies such as DLF, Tech Mahindra and IDFC public (Fortune)
→ She founded Nykaa in 2012 to sell beauty online, when people told her to pick electronics or fashion instead (Fortune)
→ Nykaa listed on 10 Nov 2021: shares opened at ₹2,018 on the NSE, a 79% premium to the ₹1,125 issue price, after the IPO was subscribed more than 82 times (Business Today)
→ That week her net worth rose to almost $7 billion, making her India's newest self-made billionaire on the Bloomberg Billionaires Index (CNN Business, 12 Nov 2021)

"I didn't know beauty, I didn't know retail. And I gave myself a chance." Falguni Nayar to Fortune, Feb 2022
