He left Microsoft to sell glasses online. Peyush Bansal's road to Lenskart, in 5 slides.

→ Finished engineering at McGill University in Canada, then worked at Microsoft in the US for close to a year (Forbes India)
→ Came back to India in November 2007, put in ₹25 lakh and started SearchMyCampus, a student classifieds site, in January 2008 (Forbes India)
→ In June 2009 the team started Flyrr, an online eyewear store for US buyers; Lenskart followed in November 2010, starting with contact lenses (Forbes India)
→ Valyoo Technologies, the company behind Lenskart, was co-founded in 2008 by Peyush Bansal, Sumeet Kapahi, Amit Chaudhary and Neha Bansal (Forbes India)
→ Lenskart's IPO raised ₹7,278.76 crore at ₹402 a share, was subscribed 28.26 times, and listed on 10 November 2025 (Business Standard)
→ His lesson: "I was always customer-obsessed but not employee-obsessed. It was a big mistake" (Forbes India, 2022)
