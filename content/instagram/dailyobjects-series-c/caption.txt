DailyObjects raises ₹332 crore in a Series C at about a ₹1,050 crore valuation.

→ Co-led by Xponentia Capital, Anicut Capital and Axiom Asia
→ Part new money, part existing investors selling shares (split not disclosed)
→ Early backer Roots Ventures sold part of its stake at a reported 18x
→ Plan: 150 exclusive stores across India in five years
→ FY25: about ₹110 crore operating revenue, about ₹16 crore net loss (Entrackr)
