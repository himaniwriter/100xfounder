Requires at least: 6.3
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.0.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Startup launch directory and news portal for 100xfounder.com.

== Description ==

* Daily import of top Product Hunt launches (top 10 by votes, top featured, 100+ upvotes).
* Finds each startup's published contact email and sends a 3-email founder outreach sequence through your own SMTP mailbox (Hostinger Email by default), with daily limits, one-click unsubscribe and reply/bounce detection over IMAP.
* Founder spotlight form, review queue and public spotlight pages.
* Instagram carousels (daily top launches + founder spotlights) rendered as PNGs with captions, ready to download and post.
* News-portal blog layout ([xf_news]), startup directory ([xf_directory]), launches ([xf_launches]) and spotlights ([xf_spotlights]).
* AdSense ready: publisher script in the head of content pages, /ads.txt, optional in-content slots, and About / Contact / Privacy / Terms / Disclaimer pages.
* Imports the old site's 1,258 startups and blog posts.

Fonts: Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License, see assets/fonts/OFL.txt).
