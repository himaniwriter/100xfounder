# Copyright takedown: what to do when someone copies 100xFounder

Policy page: https://100xfounder.com/copyright-policy/

## Steps
1. **Save proof.** Screenshot the copy with its date, and save its URL and an archive.org snapshot. Note our original URL and publish date (from the post or the sitemap lastmod).
2. **Ask the site first.** Use the friendly notice below. Wait 5 business days.
3. **Hosting provider.** Look up the host (`whois`, or a "who is hosting" lookup) and send the formal DMCA notice to its abuse address.
4. **Google Search.** File the copied URLs at Google's "Report content on Google" legal removal form (choose Copyright). Do the same for Bing's copyright infringement form.
5. **Platforms.** Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube and Medium each have their own copyright report form. Use the formal notice text below.
6. **Keep a log** of every notice: date, recipient, URL and outcome.

Only file for content that is ours: our own text, our graphics and our calculators. Facts, short quotes with credit, and images we used under someone else's licence aren't ours to claim. A false notice can create liability (17 U.S.C. § 512(f)).

## Friendly notice
> Subject: Content copied from 100xfounder.com
>
> Hi, the page at [THEIR URL] reproduces our article [OUR URL], published on [DATE], without permission. Please remove it, or replace it with a short excerpt and a link to the original, within 5 business days. Our copyright policy: https://100xfounder.com/copyright-policy/
>
> 100xFounder editorial team

## Formal DMCA notice (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))
> Subject: DMCA takedown notice
>
> 1. Copyrighted work: the article "[TITLE]" at [OUR URL], first published [DATE].
> 2. Infringing material: [THEIR URL(S)]
> 3. Contact: [NAME OF COPYRIGHT OWNER OR AUTHORISED AGENT], [POSTAL ADDRESS], [PHONE], [EMAIL]
> 4. I have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
> 5. The information in this notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorised to act on behalf of the owner, of the copyright allegedly infringed.
> 6. Signature: [FULL NAME], [DATE]

The notice must be signed by the copyright owner or someone authorised to act for them, with real contact details. Hosts and Google reject notices that don't have them.
