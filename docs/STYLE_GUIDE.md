# 100Xfounder style guide

## Voice
- Clear, specific and calm, like a smart friend who works in startups. No hype words (revolutionary, game-changing) and no exclamation marks.
- Indian context first, explained for a global reader: show ₹ with a $ equivalent where useful, and explain Indian terms such as LPA and Cr once.

## Structure (news)
1. **Headline:** who + what + the number, 55–70 characters. Example: "Zepto raises $200M Series D led by StepStone Group".
2. **Opening paragraph:** the whole story in two sentences: who, what, how much, from whom, and why now.
3. **Body:** H2 sections on the details, the use of funds or the product, the market context and competitors.
4. **"Why it matters":** 2–4 sentences of our own analysis.
5. **Length:** 350–700 words. Long reads run 1,200–2,500.

## Facts
- Every number, date, quote and claim comes from a listed source. Write "according to" for single-source claims.
- Primary sources first. Note when a figure is "reported" rather than confirmed.
- Net worth, valuations and revenue only when published by a reliable source, with the date.

## Formatting
- Sentence-case H2s. Short paragraphs of 2–4 sentences.
- Lists only for real lists.
- Link to company pages on our site where they exist, for example `/startup/slug/`.
- Use images only when we have the rights: company press kits, Unsplash or our own. Always add a credit.
